Phase 1+2

Email Sequences - Bradley Leavitt

Phase 1 + 2 deliverable · Welcome flow (8 touches), pre-call nurture (3 touches), post-call follow-up (4 touches over 14 days).

Sequence 1 - Welcome Flow (10 days, 8 touches)

Triggered when a new subscriber finishes the AFT Project Scorecard. Segmented by score tier (0-40 / 41-70 / 71-100).

T+0 - Immediate (Score Result)

Subject: Your AFT Project Scorecard - score [X/100] + what it means for your operation Body: Score reveal, 1-paragraph context on what the score means for a GC at their revenue stage, 3-bullet action plan specific to their segment, link to download printable PDF summary, soft CTA toward the AFT Operations Playbook. Goal: deliver the promised result, set expectations for the next 10 days.

T+1 day - The Project Management Story

Subject: The scheduling mistake that cost us 3 weeks on a $1.2M build Body: A specific story from an AFT Construction project - a real operational failure and how the current AFT system was built to prevent it. Closes with "the AFT Operations Playbook is the documented version of that fix." Goal: bridge build-watcher → operator who needs the system.

T+3 days - The Vendor Story

Subject: The vendor relationship that saved us $80K on a single project Body: Specific story about a vendor/specification decision that shifted margin on a real AFT build. The Kohler/Pella framework teaser. CTA toward AFT Operations Playbook landing. Goal: prove operational depth beyond what appears on IG.

T+5 days - The Subcontractor Story

Subject: Why we fired our best subcontractor (and what fixed the next one) Body: A specific sub-management story. The retention and accountability framework that came out of it. Vulnerable framing. CTA toward course landing. Goal: build trust through admitted operational mistakes.

T+7 days - The Positioning Story

Subject: What happened when we stopped taking projects under $800K Body: The decision to move fully luxury, the fear going in, the revenue impact on the other side. "The Operations Playbook is the framework that made that transition possible." CTA toward course. Goal: route the $1M-$5M GC operator who wants to move upmarket into the buying frame.

T+9 days - Soft Pitch Open

Subject: Quick question before I disappear from your inbox Body: Two-line check-in. "Is the Playbook a fit for where you're at right now?" Link to landing. "Or hit reply and tell me what's actually costing you time on your current projects." Goal: segment hot vs warm; route hot to landing, warm to a 1:1 reply for triage.

T+10 days - Last Touch (segmented)

  • Score 71-100 (Mastermind-ready): "You don't need the Playbook first. You're ready for the AFT Mastermind." Routes to mastermind application/waitlist.
  • Score 41-70 (Course pool): Last call for $497 Playbook tier. CTA toward course.
  • Score 0-40 (Foundations): "Start with the Scorecard action plan. Then come back." Sequences into a 60-day re-engagement at a lower frequency.

Sequence 2 - Pre-Call Nurture (3 touches before discovery call)

Triggered when an AFT Operations Playbook buyer books the optional 1:1 strategy add-on, or when someone applies to the AFT Mastermind.

T-7 days

Subject: Before our call - three things I'll want to know about your operation Body: The three questions Bradley will ask in advance - revenue stage, biggest current constraint, most recent project that didn't go to plan. Helps the applicant arrive with context ready.

T-3 days

Subject: What I've learned coaching GC operators in your revenue band Body: A pattern from working with $1M-$5M GC operators. One insight specific to the stage they're at. Sets the frame for the call.

T-1 day

Subject: Tomorrow at [time] - one thing to bring Body: Calendar reminder, one specific prep ask (P&L from last quarter, current active project list, sub list).

Sequence 3 - Post-Call Follow-Up (4 touches over 14 days)

Triggered after the discovery call regardless of outcome.

T+1 hour - Recap

Subject: Recap from our call + two next steps Body: 1-paragraph recap, two specific action items Bradley committed to on the call, link to the agreed-upon path (Playbook / Mastermind / waitlist).

T+3 days - The Specific Resource

Subject: That [framework] I mentioned on the call Body: Whatever specific resource was referenced on the call (site management template, vendor scorecard, pricing model framework). Sent as clean PDF or shared Notion page.

T+7 days - The Check-In

Subject: Did you implement [X] yet? Body: Soft accountability nudge. "Reply with a one-line update if you did. Reply with a question if you hit a wall."

T+14 days - The Decision

Subject: Where you landed Body: Clean ask: "Are we moving forward with [agreed path] or holding off?" No pressure. Clear decision frame. Either route triggers the right next sequence (onboarding or long-term nurture).

Subject Line Bank (rotation)

For ongoing broadcasts beyond the welcome sequence:

  • "What I'd run if I were starting AFT Construction in this market"
  • "Three numbers I read every Monday on active projects"
  • "The Friday close-out rule"
  • "Why I told my best sub to raise his prices"
  • "The IBS talk most GC operators needed to hear"
  • "What a $2M build and a $200K build have in common (and what they don't)"
  • "Craft without systems is just chaos with nice photos"

Triggers + Tags

  • Tag by score tier on quiz completion
  • Tag by purchase (Playbook $497 / $997 / Mastermind application)
  • Tag by event source (IBS-2026, podcast-[name], IG-organic)
  • Tag by content engagement (opened 5+ in 30 days = "warm operator")
  • Auto-suppress promotional during IBS week (replace with event-specific track)
  • Re-engagement campaign at 60 days of inactivity
