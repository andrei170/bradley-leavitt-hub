Foundation

Copy Strategy - Bradley Leavitt

Foundation doc · Voice, brand pillars, headline bank, framing AG1 will write to.

Voice

Craft-first, operator-direct, anti-hype. Bradley's @aft_construction content shows the work without commentary or motivational framing. The voice respects the trade - never talks down to contractors, never uses coaching-world language, never positions as a guru. The AFT brand is built on documentation of real luxury builds. The copy should feel like it was written by someone who's been on job sites, not a conference stage.

Use: operator, builder, system, framework, project, install, spec, bid, margin, crew, scope, deliver, standard, quality, process, field-tested Avoid: mindset, transformation, unlock, game-changer, journey, breakthrough, abundance, level up, hustle, scaling (without specific mechanism), coach (as identity)

Brand Pillars

  1. AFT Standard - the AFT Construction brand is built on doing luxury custom home building at a level most GCs don't reach. Every deliverable should reflect that the standard of the work is the differentiator, not the marketing.
  2. Craft-First - this is built by a builder for builders. No theory, no "as a business coach who's never poured a footer" language. Everything comes from the job site.
  3. Systems Over Hustle - Bradley's operational credibility (Inc. 5000, multiple active luxury builds simultaneously) comes from systems, not working harder. Copy reflects that.
  4. Operator Peer Register - the audience is GC operators in the field. The copy treats them as peers running real businesses, not students learning a trade.

Headline Bank

For the AFT Operations Playbook landing page, hub callouts, and ad creative:

  • Run a luxury GC operation that doesn't depend on you being on every job site.
  • The AFT system. Built over 10 years of luxury custom home builds. Now documented.
  • Six modules. Built by a builder. For $1M-$5M GC operators.
  • Inc. 5000. Kohler ambassador. IBS speaker. Now: the operational framework.
  • What I'd build if I were starting AFT Construction again today.
  • The project management system behind 289K @aft_construction followers.
  • From first luxury spec home to recognised brand. The documented path.
  • Vendor relationships, client experience, luxury positioning. In that order.

Framing for the Funnel

  • Top of funnel (scorecard, hooks, content): operation-pain led, not vanity-led. "Your RFI process is bleeding time, your vendor lead times are killing margin, your sub vetting process is leaving callbacks on every job" - real GC problems beat lifestyle hooks.
  • Mid funnel (course landing, VSL): AFT Construction as the proof. The brand was built on these systems. The Playbook is the documented version.
  • High funnel (mastermind application path): operator-to-operator framing. "If the Playbook is the foundation, the Mastermind is the room where we build the next floor together."

Banned Phrases

Universal AG1 ban list, plus Bradley-specific: - No "blueprint" (overused in GC/builder coaching niche) - No "secrets" or "hacks" - No "unlock" - No "transform" unless paired with a concrete operational metric - No first-month-lift language without the client-context caveat - No "guru" framing or any suggestion Bradley is a business coach rather than a GC operator

Tone Calibration By Surface

Surface Register Length
IG hook Specific to a real GC operation problem 1 line
Ad copy Process-led, claim + qualifier 2-3 lines
Email subject Direct, question or curiosity, never clickbait 5-9 words
VSL opening "AFT Construction started as one crew, one market, one standard. Here's what the standard actually costs to maintain - and why it's worth it." 1-2 sentences
Landing hero What's at stake for a $1M-$5M GC operator 1 promise + 1 mechanism
FAQ Plain answers, no hedge 2-3 sentences each

Voice Test

Every paragraph passes if a working GC operator reading it nods, not laughs. If it could appear in a generic "scale your business online" course, rewrite it. If it references something that only makes sense to someone who has managed a luxury custom build from site prep to handover, it's probably right.
