Tech Stack (recommended, swappable)

  • CMS / Course host: Kajabi (preferred - all-in-one) or Teachable
  • Landing pages: Webflow (control) or Kajabi (speed)
  • Email: Kit / ConvertKit (preferred for operator audiences) or Kajabi-native
  • Quiz / Scorecard: ScoreApp (preferred for scoring + segmentation) or Typeform
  • Payment: Stripe (course) + Whop (mastermind membership)
  • Video host: Wistia (course) + YouTube (long-form) + Loom (1:1 personal video reviews)
  • Analytics: GA4 + Meta Pixel + Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps)
  • CRM: GHL (preferred for contractor/trade audiences) or Kajabi-native
  • Community / Mastermind: Skool (preferred - low overhead) or Circle
  • Scheduling: Calendly (default) or Cal.com
  • Project / build: Notion (handover-friendly) + Linear (engineering)

Integration Points

  1. Scorecard → Email: ScoreApp triggers Kit on quiz completion with score tag
  2. Email → Course: Kit triggers Kajabi enrolment on purchase webhook
  3. Course → Mastermind application: Course graduates routed to AFT Mastermind apply form on completion of module 6
  4. IBS/event QR → Scorecard → Email: Live event attendees route through QR to Scorecard, auto-tagged as "IBS-2026" for segmentation
  5. All events → GA4 + Meta Pixel via GTM: captures full funnel attribution
  6. CRM → Email: GHL syncs with Kit so 1:1 conversations land in the same record

Owner Map

  • Bradley: filming (VSL + photoshoot day), monthly live Q&A on the course, AFT Systems YouTube content, top-line decisions on copy + brand
  • AG1 (Andrei): everything else - landing page build, email writing, automation setup, content production cycles, ads management, weekly reporting

Failure Modes + Mitigations

  • VSL filming delays → use existing @aft_construction YouTube content as Phase 1 placeholder; replace with custom VSL by end of Phase 1
  • IBS/event integration delay → run digital QR capture first via bio link; physical event integration follows the next speaking appearance
  • Mastermind fill time → open pre-launch waitlist at Phase 2 completion; first cohort fills from course graduates + IBS attendees + scorecard "mastermind-ready" tier
  • Scorecard over-segmentation → start with 3 tiers; expand to 5 only after 500 quiz completions confirms volume by tier

KPIs (90-day)

  • Phase 1: AFT Operations Playbook landing live, 20+ buyers in first 30 days
  • Phase 2: 500+ scorecard completions, 5-10% IG-to-email conversion
  • Phase 3: 40+ short-form posts shipped, 2 AFT Systems YouTube episodes published
  • Phase 4: Meta CPL under $45, ROAS 3x by week 12
  • Year-1 floor target: $280-800K incremental on top of existing AFT Construction service revenue and brand deals
