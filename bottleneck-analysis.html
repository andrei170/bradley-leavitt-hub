Three Bottlenecks I Found Inside Your Funnel - Bradley Leavitt

All three install in less than 7 days from kickoff. Built to compound. Order by impact.

Snapshot

  • 289K IG @aft_construction + YouTube build-process library + Inc. 5000 + Phoenix Titan 100 HOF + Kohler/Pella ambassador + IBS speaker
  • AFT Construction = local Scottsdale luxury GC service. Only monetisation path for 289K followers is "hire AFT to build your house."
  • Comment sections on IG and YouTube are full of GC operators asking process questions - organic demand for a course/mastermind that doesn't exist
  • Zero mid-tier between following @aft_construction and hiring AFT Construction for a $1M+ custom build
  • No lead magnet, no quiz, no email capture for the builder/operator audience
  • The entire operator education gap is unmonetised

Bottleneck #1 - 289K builder audience follows @aft_construction with zero path to spend money

The asymmetry: @aft_construction has 289K followers. The audience skews toward GC operators, luxury home designers, and builders who study the content to improve their own projects. They comment with specific process questions. They watch the build documentation to learn. Right now there is no on-ramp from "follows @aft_construction" to "buys Bradley's operator system." The conversion path from engaged follower to paying operator is broken because there is no product.

A $497-$997 "AFT Operations Playbook" course translating Bradley's luxury GC framework into install-ready systems for $1M-$5M contractors gives the follower somewhere to spend money well below the threshold of hiring AFT for a $1M+ custom build.

Why it's costing him: - 289K followers watching build content with no path to a $497-$997 product = tens of thousands of unqualified-to-buy operators leaving every month with knowledge but no spend - 0.2-0.5% of the engaged follower base converting to a $497 course = 290-580 incremental Year-1 buyers from existing organic reach alone - Course buyers become qualified mastermind applicants 6-12 months later (warm pipeline into the high-ticket) - Currently IG reach is the last touch for operators - no email capture, no retargeting, no segmentation

The fix: - "AFT Operations Playbook" - 6-module self-paced course - Modules: luxury project management system, vendor relationship framework, client experience architecture, luxury positioning for $1M-$5M GCs, subcontractor management and quality control, scaling from regional GC to recognised brand - Includes monthly live Q&A with Bradley (90 min) - $497 standard, $997 with personal video review of buyer's current GC operation

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $150-400K/yr at 300-800 buyers x $497-$997.

Why this is #1: Largest ceiling. Largest warm audience to convert (289K @aft_construction followers). Compounds into mastermind fill rate - course buyers turn into high-ticket applicants. Every month the course doesn't exist is a month the operator audience routes to a competitor who does have one.

Bottleneck #2 - No lead magnet or scorecard between IG follow and $1M+ custom build quote

The leak: The 289K @aft_construction audience has nowhere to convert to email. They can either contact aftconstruction.com (homeowner-side, wrong brand for GC operators) or keep consuming free content indefinitely. There is no soft entry point. No quiz, no scorecard, no audit, no free resource.

An "AFT Project Scorecard" quiz - 8 questions, returns a 0-100 score, captures email, segments by revenue stage and primary operational challenge - converts the casual follower into a tracked email subscriber Bradley can market to for life.

Why it's costing him: - 5-10% IG-to-email is standard in trades with the right lead magnet = 15K-29K incremental email subscribers from the existing IG base alone - Without segmentation, Bradley can't isolate "$1M-$5M GC operators" (mastermind feeder pool) from "$500K-$1M GCs" (course pool) from "designers/homeowners" (content-only, wrong product) - No email list = no nurture = no pre-sell of course launches or mastermind cohorts - Every week without an opt-in, followers who would have bought a course are routing to trade competitors who have lead magnets and nurture sequences

The fix: - "The AFT Project Scorecard" - 8-question diagnostic on ScoreApp / Typeform - Captures email, returns custom PDF action plan based on score band - Segments by: revenue stage, project type (luxury vs volume), biggest bottleneck, team size - Triggers 8-touch nurture sequence routed by segment (course pool vs mastermind-ready)

Estimated impact: Year-1 lift $50-150K/yr incremental from email-driven course sales and mastermind fill rate. Compounds for life of the list.

Why this is #2: Foundational for #1 and #3. Fast install (less than 7 days). Compounds infinitely - the email list is the asset every other bottleneck rides on. Without it, course launches rely entirely on IG post reach.

Bottleneck #3 - Inc. 5000 and IBS speaker credentials are trust signals with no conversion surface

The contradiction: Bradley has Inc. 5000 recognition, Phoenix Titan 100 HOF, Kohler and Pella ambassadorship, and IBS speaking credentials. These are signals that the AFT operational model is validated at the highest level of the industry. Right now they appear in bios and press - not in a conversion surface. There is no mastermind or group coaching offer that capitalises on these credentials with the operator audience who already follows Bradley specifically because of this trust stack.

An "AFT Mastermind" - quarterly cohort for $1M-$5M GC operators ($3K-$5K/quarter or $10K-$15K annual) with direct access to Bradley's project management framework, vendor relationships, and luxury positioning system - converts the trust stack into a recurring revenue layer that doesn't exist.

Why it's costing him: - IBS attendees and AFT followers who are $1M-$5M GC operators have no way to work with Bradley below the $1M+ custom home threshold - The Inc. 5000 and Titan 100 credentials are unused in the monetisation stack - they generate credibility without a product to attach that credibility to - Quarterly cohort model (8-12 operators per cohort) at $10K-$15K/yr = $80K-$180K/yr ARR from one cohort; scales with each IBS speaking engagement as a feeder event - The comment sections on IG and YouTube are self-selecting applicants for this offer. They already exist. There is no form to collect them.

The fix: - "AFT Mastermind" - quarterly cohort model for $1M-$5M GC operators - Monthly group call with Bradley (90 min, working session not lecture) - Private community (Skool or Circle) - Vendor relationship framework access (Kohler/Pella contact relationships, specification frameworks) - IBS pre-cohort intake (leverages speaker appearances as intake events) - $10K-$15K/yr or $3K-$5K/quarter per operator

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $80-250K/yr ARR at 8-25 operators x $10K-$15K/yr. Compounds with each IBS cohort and course-graduate feeder pool.

Why this is #3: Smaller ceiling than #1 in Year-1 because it requires course infrastructure (#1) and email list (#2) to build the warm feeder pool. But it is the highest-LTV layer and the one that uses Bradley's IBS speaking and brand credentials most directly.
