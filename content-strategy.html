Phase 3

Content Strategy - Bradley Leavitt

Phase 3 deliverable · Hook bank, photoshoot brief, short-form roadmap, and channel strategy aligned to the AFT Operations Playbook and Scorecard funnel.

Premise

Bradley's 289K @aft_construction audience is engaged but unmonetised. The content currently documents luxury build projects at a high production level. None of it routes a viewer to a clear next step for operators who want to learn the AFT system. Phase 3 builds a content layer with one job: get the right contractor/operator viewer onto the AFT Project Scorecard.

The content strategy does not require Bradley to become a business coach or leave the trade register. Every hook, every short-form piece, every YouTube video stays craft-first. The conversion layer is invisible to the audience - they get process knowledge, the Scorecard gets the email.

Hook Bank - 40 Hooks

Eight categories of operator-pain hooks. Each pulls a different segment of the 289K toward the scorecard.

Project Management Pain (5)

  1. The scheduling mistake that cost us 3 weeks on a $1.2M build.
  2. The subcontractor vetting process we use before anyone gets on a job site.
  3. How we manage 4 active luxury builds simultaneously without a site manager on every one.
  4. The pre-pour checklist that has never failed us in 10 years.
  5. Why your RFI process is probably the slowest thing on your job site.

Vendor and Specification (5)

  1. The Kohler specification process we run on every AFT master bathroom.
  2. How to get Pella window lead times under 6 weeks when everyone else is waiting 14.
  3. The vendor relationship that saved us $80K on a single project last year.
  4. Why we never spec a product we can't get a rep on the phone for.
  5. The materials allowance system that stops client change orders before they start.

Luxury Client Experience (5)

  1. The pre-contract client alignment meeting that eliminates 90% of scope creep.
  2. What we send every client after the frame walk that no other GC sends.
  3. The Friday update format we've used for every AFT project since year two.
  4. Why we over-communicate on price and under-communicate on timeline.
  5. The selection meeting format that turns a two-hour decision into a 45-minute one.

Luxury Positioning for GCs (5)

  1. How a GC in a mid-tier market moves to luxury without losing their current clients.
  2. The portfolio photography process we use that gets us Inc. 5000 press.
  3. Why we stopped taking any project under $800K and what happened to revenue.
  4. The one brand decision that separated AFT from every other GC in Scottsdale.
  5. How to price for luxury margin without losing the bid to a cheaper competitor.

Team and Subcontractor Management (5)

  1. The performance conversation we have with every sub at the 30-day mark.
  2. Why we use the same 12 subcontractors on every project and how we found them.
  3. The weekly site meeting that keeps our foremen accountable without micromanaging.
  4. What happens when a sub's work fails inspection on an AFT project.
  5. The subcontractor scorecard we created that cut our callbacks by 60%.

Business Operations (5)

  1. The financial model we use to project cash flow 90 days out on every active project.
  2. How we handle the gap between draws and payables without touching a line of credit.
  3. The one hire that changed how AFT scales - and it wasn't a project manager.
  4. Why our overhead is lower at $8M in revenue than it was at $2M.
  5. The quarterly business review format we run as a two-person leadership team.

Build Documentation and Content (5)

  1. Why we started filming every build and what it did to our inbound lead quality.
  2. The shot list we give our video team for every new project start.
  3. How 289K followers on a construction account changes how clients value you.
  4. The Instagram strategy that generated more qualified leads than any ad we've run.
  5. Why showing the hard days on camera builds more trust than the highlight reels.

Industry and Craft (5)

  1. What the IBS show gets right about the luxury build market (and what it misses).
  2. The three trends in luxury home building that every GC needs to know for 2026.
  3. Why custom home clients in the $1M-$3M range have completely different psychology than $3M+ clients.
  4. The spec home versus custom home decision - how we think about it and when we do each.
  5. What the Inc. 5000 process taught us about running a construction business.

Short-Form Roadmap (90 days)

  • Weeks 4-6: post 5 hooks/week from Categories 1-3 (project management, vendor specification, luxury client experience) - the highest-pain operator triggers
  • Weeks 7-9: post 5/week from Categories 4-6 (luxury positioning, team management, business operations)
  • Weeks 10-12: post 5/week from Categories 7-8 (build documentation, industry/craft) + retargeting ads layered on top of organic

Cadence: 5 IG reels + 5 YT shorts + 1 long-form per week. Repurpose ratio 1:5 - one filmed asset = one long-form episode + 5 short cuts.

YouTube Strategy

Bradley's YouTube channel is already generating long-form build documentation content. Phase 3 adds a second track:

Track 1 (existing): Full build documentation. Groundbreaking to completion. Keep producing. This is the brand anchor.

Track 2 (new): "AFT Systems" series. 12-15 minute single-topic deep-dives on specific operational questions that appear repeatedly in the comment sections. "How We Manage Multiple Active Luxury Builds" / "The Vendor Relationship System Behind AFT Construction" / "How We Price Luxury Custom Homes - The Full Model." Each video ends with a CTA to the AFT Project Scorecard.

Every Track 2 video gets 5 short-form cuts for IG reels and YT shorts.

Photoshoot Brief

Two-day shoot. Ideal timing: Week 5-6 of Phase 3.

Day 1 - On Location, Active Build Site: Bradley in site gear at an active AFT luxury custom home project. Hard hat, work boots, clipboard. Natural light where possible. B-roll of luxury finishes being installed (tile work, custom cabinetry, windows, framing). 200+ stills, 3-5 hours of B-roll. This is the trust-building visual library for course landing pages and ad creative.

Day 2 - Office/Studio Setup: Bradley at his desk, blueprints or project management materials visible, AFT branding in background. 150+ stills, 2-3 hours of talking-head B-roll for IG cutdowns and VSL pre-roll.

Outputs feed: AFT Operations Playbook landing page, Scorecard result page, 40-hook IG content calendar, ad creative library, AFT Mastermind waitlist page.

Conversion Goal

Every piece of content has the same final CTA: "AFT Project Scorecard - 8 questions, custom action plan for your GC business, in your inbox" - with the link in bio routing through Linktree to the Scorecard quiz.

Channel Priority

  1. Instagram @aft_construction - primary distribution. Reels are the primary short-form format.
  2. YouTube @aft_construction - long-form documentation and AFT Systems series. Second-highest conversion channel.
  3. Podcast guest appearances - each appearance ends with AFT Project Scorecard CTA.
  4. IBS and industry events - live capture via QR code to Scorecard on stage.
