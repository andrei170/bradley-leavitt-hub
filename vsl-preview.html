VSL Preview Script - Bradley Leavitt

Phase 1 deliverable · Script structure, page layout, and production notes for the AFT Operations Playbook hero VSL.

Format

Short preview VSL only. 4-6 minute talking-head with B-roll inserts, hosted on Wistia and embedded in the landing-page hero. Purpose: establish authority, demonstrate operational depth, and route the viewer to the application form below the video. A full 18-22 minute VSL can follow once the first cohort validates demand.

Cold Open (00:00 - 00:30)

On camera, Bradley at his desk or on an active job site:

"AFT Construction has been in the luxury custom home market for over a decade. We've built everything from $800K spec homes to $5M custom builds. We have 289K followers watching us document the work. And the question I get more than any other - more than which windows we spec, more than how we handle permit delays, more than anything - is: how do you run it? Not the builds. The operation. How do you manage four active luxury projects at the same time without missing anything? That's what this course is."

The Stakes (00:30 - 02:00)

What the average $1M-$5M GC operator is actually dealing with:

  • Sub shows up late to a critical inspection window. Third time this year.
  • Material lead times blew the schedule by two weeks. Client is asking for answers.
  • Change order came in at $40K over allowance. The conversation wasn't going to be easy.
  • Friday afternoon is spent chasing questions that should have been answered on Monday.
  • The margin was supposed to be 22%. The actual came in at 14%.

The AFT Operations Playbook is the documented system that turned those problems into processes. Not theory. Field-tested on real luxury projects, real budgets, real clients.

The Credential (02:00 - 03:00)

Quick-hit, not chronological:

  • AFT Construction, Scottsdale - over a decade of luxury custom home builds
  • Inc. 5000 honoree
  • Phoenix Titan 100 Hall of Fame
  • Kohler and Pella brand ambassador
  • IBS speaker

The point is not the accolades. The point is the system that generated them - and that system is now documented.

The Six Modules (03:00 - 04:30)

Walk through each module, 10-15 seconds each:

  1. The AFT Project Management System - how to run four active luxury builds without a site manager on every one
  2. The Vendor Relationship Framework - how to build spec relationships with Kohler, Pella, and premium materials suppliers that reduce lead times and improve quality control
  3. The Luxury Client Experience Architecture - the pre-contract alignment, the weekly communication format, the handover process that generates referrals
  4. Luxury Positioning for $1M-$5M GCs - how to move your operation upmarket without losing your current client base
  5. Subcontractor Management and Quality Control - the vetting process, the performance framework, the accountability system that cuts callbacks
  6. Scaling From Regional GC to Recognised Brand - what actually drives brand value in the luxury build market, and how we built @aft_construction to 289K

Who This Is For (04:30 - 05:15)

This is for: $1M-$5M GC operators who are already doing real work and want to systemise how they do it. Operators who follow @aft_construction and want the operational framework behind the brand. Contractors ready to move into luxury or already there who want to run it at the AFT standard.

This is not for: solo operators under $500K (the systems are premature at that stage). Operators looking for marketing tactics (this is operational depth, not lead generation). GCs already past $10M with established systems (the AFT Mastermind would be the right room, not the Playbook).

Close (05:15 - 06:00)

The application form is below this video. Four minutes to complete. We open the course quarterly and cap cohorts to keep the live Q&A sessions real and useful.

"I spent ten years building the AFT system on real projects with real clients and real consequences. The Playbook is the documented version of that decade. The application is below."

Production Notes

  • Filming location: Active AFT job site (talking head in site gear, B-roll of luxury finishes) + AFT office or desk setup (talking head, more polished)
  • Camera: primary + B-camera + lav mic. Sony FX3 or equivalent. Rode Wireless Pro for audio.
  • Wardrobe: AFT branded gear or clean site attire for job-site talking head; cleaner look for desk setup
  • Lighting: natural at job site; key + fill for desk talking head
  • Pace: target 1.0x watchable at all times. B-roll cuts every 15-20 seconds
  • Music: none under talking head. Light architectural/minimal bed under B-roll only
  • Captions: burnt-in for IG / YT shorts; SRT for Wistia long-form
  • Thumbnail (Wistia poster): Bradley on a luxury job site, looking at camera. Clean architectural background. Title overlay: "The AFT Operations Playbook."
